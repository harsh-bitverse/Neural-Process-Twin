## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone 08B.2 was commissioned to resolve the conflicting out-of-distribution (OOD) subgroup metrics reported in Milestones 08B and 08B.1 before commencing multi-resolution neural operator research (Milestone 08C+).

Specifically, Milestone 08B reported that in short-correlation Gaussian Random Field (GRF) microstructures, baseline Fourier Neural Operator (FNO-2D) Candidate A0 exhibited a boundary flux imbalance of **$90,192.2\text{ W}$** (seed 42), averaging **$63,034.1\text{ W}$** across seeds, and Candidate A1 achieved a $60.7\%$ reduction down to **$35,471.2\text{ W}$**. Conversely, Milestone 08B.1 reported GRF flux imbalances of **$24,301.2\text{ W}$** for A0 and **$9,595.7\text{ W}$** for A1. Furthermore, historical milestones (05B, 06A, 06B, 08A) repeatedly cited an authoritative baseline anomaly of **$59,591.9\text{ W}$**.

Through bit-level artifact hashing, mathematical recomputation, dataset provenance tracing, and code archaeology, this audit has established the **exact mathematical and algorithmic root causes** of every discrepancy:

1. **Root Cause of the 08B vs. 08B.1 Subgroup Discrepancy (Sample Misclassification)**:
   - In `scaled_dataset.h5`, samples in `test_ood` ($N=200$) were generated by a Bernoulli($p=0.7$) process, resulting in an **interleaved mixture** of $153$ High-Contrast Inclusions ($k_{\max}/k_{\min} \ge 20.0$) and $47$ Short-Correlation GRF samples ($k_{\max}/k_{\min} < 20.0$).
   - Milestone 08B correctly filtered samples by physical attribute (`contrast >= 20.0` vs. `contrast < 20.0`).
   - Milestone 08B.1's independent evaluation script (`run_milestone_08b1_evaluation.py`) mistakenly used sequential array slicing: `0:153` for High-Contrast and `153:200` for GRF.
   - Slicing `[153:200]` captured **$36$ High-Contrast Inclusion samples ($76.6\%$) and only $11$ GRF samples ($23.4\%$)**. Because inclusion microstructures exhibit far lower flux errors than GRFs, this accidental dilution artificially suppressed the reported "GRF" imbalance in 08B.1 from $90,192.2\text{ W}$ to $24,301.2\text{ W}$ (A0) and from $35,471.2\text{ W}$ to $9,595.7\text{ W}$ (A1).
2. **Provenance of the Authoritative $59,591.9\text{ W}$ Anomaly**:
   - The figure $59,591.9\text{ W}$ originates from Milestone 05B, where the **5-member Deep Ensemble average prediction** ($\bar{T} = \frac{1}{5}\sum_{s=42}^{46} T^{(s)}$) was evaluated on the $47$ canonical GRF samples.
   - Re-evaluating the frozen 5-member ensemble on the 47 canonical GRF samples yields **$59,591.9\text{ W}$** (reproduced to within $0.02\text{ W}$), with an accompanying relative $L_2$ error of **$14.26\%$** and high-contrast imbalance of **$6,781.9\text{ W}$**, matching Milestone 05B verbatim.
   - Individual ensemble members exhibit high variance on GRFs: Seed 42 ($90,192.2\text{ W}$), Seed 43 ($48,324.4\text{ W}$), Seed 44 ($50,585.6\text{ W}$), Seed 45 ($85,004.6\text{ W}$), and Seed 46 ($27,075.5\text{ W}$), with an arithmetic mean across the five seeds of $60,236.5\text{ W}$.
3. **Canonical Protocol & Test Suite**:
   - Implemented an authoritative evaluation runner ([`scripts/evaluate_canonical_metrics.py`](file:///c:/Workspace/Projects/Neural%20Process%20Twin/scripts/evaluate_canonical_metrics.py)) and regression test suite ([`tests/scientific/test_canonical_evaluation_reconciliation.py`](file:///c:/Workspace/Projects/Neural%20Process%20Twin/tests/scientific/test_canonical_evaluation_reconciliation.py)). ## 3. Phase 2 — Historical Results Reconciliation and Root-Cause Analysis

### 3.1 Mechanistic Root-Cause of the 08B vs 08B.1 Discrepancy

In `src/neural_process_twin/data/orchestration.py`, the generator synthesizes `test_ood` samples using a per-sample Bernoulli draw:
```python
if split == SplitName.TEST_OOD:
    if rng.random() < 0.7:
        # Composite material with contrast ratio 20:1 to 50:1 (High-Contrast Inclusions)
        k_field = comp_gen.generate(...)
    else:
        # Short correlation length Matérn GRF (ell in [0.04, 0.08] m)
        k_field = grf_gen.generate(...)
```
Consequently, samples are **randomly interleaved throughout indices 0 to 199**:
- **Sample 0**: Short-Correlation GRF (`contrast = 15.68 < 20.0`)
- **Sample 1**: High-Contrast Inclusion (`contrast = 34.22 >= 20.0`)
- **Sample 2**: High-Contrast Inclusion (`contrast = 41.05 >= 20.0`)
- **Sample 10**: Short-Correlation GRF (`contrast = 18.21 < 20.0`)

Across all 200 samples, there are exactly **153 High-Contrast samples ($76.5\%$)** and **47 GRF samples ($23.5\%$)**.

#### Comparison of Evaluation Implementations:

1. **Milestone 08B Implementation** ([`scripts/run_milestone_08b_experiments.py`](file:///c:/Workspace/Projects/Neural%20Process%20Twin/scripts/run_milestone_08b_experiments.py#L339-L342)):
   ```python
   contrasts = np.array(contrast_list)
   high_mask = contrasts >= 20.0
   grf_mask = contrasts < 20.0
   ```
   *Result*: Evaluated the exact 153 High-Contrast and 47 GRF samples based on physical conductivity fields.

2. **Milestone 08B.1 Implementation** ([`scripts/run_milestone_08b1_evaluation.py`](file:///c:/Workspace/Projects/Neural%20Process%20Twin/scripts/run_milestone_08b1_evaluation.py#L234-L236)):
   ```python
   hc_indices = list(range(153))
   grf_indices = list(range(153, 200))
   ```
   *Result*: Evaluated index slices `[0:153]` and `[153:200]`.

#### Composition of Slices:
- **Slice `[153:200]` (Labeled "GRF" in 08B.1)**:
  Contains **36 High-Contrast Inclusion samples ($76.6\%$)** and **only 11 GRF samples ($23.4\%$)**.
- **Slice `[0:153]` (Labeled "High-Contrast" in 08B.1)**:
  Contains **117 High-Contrast Inclusion samples ($76.5\%$)** and **36 GRF samples ($23.5\%$)**.

Because High-Contrast Inclusions have smooth matrix boundaries with localized inclusions, their boundary flux error is an order of magnitude smaller than GRF microstructures ($7.0\text{ kW}$ vs. $90.2\text{ kW}$ in A0). When slice `[153:200]` was averaged in 08B.1, the 36 inclusion samples dominated the mean, pulling the reported "GRF" flux imbalance down from $90,192.2\text{ W}$ to $24,301.2\text{ W}$.

## 4. Phase 3 — Canonical Metric Specification

To ensure future milestones adhere to a unified, unambiguous mathematical convention, Neural Process Twin establishes the following **authoritative evaluation protocol**.

### 4.1 Per-Sample Metrics

For a 2D Cartesian uniform grid of $N_x \times N_y$ cells with cell dimensions $\Delta x, \Delta y$ and thickness $W_{\text{slab}}$:

1. **Cell-Centred Temperature Errors**:
   - $\text{RMSE}_i = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N_{\text{cells}}} \sum_{j=0}^{N_y-1} \sum_{k=0}^{N_x-1} \left(T_{i, j, k}^{\text{pred}} - T_{i, j, k}^{\text{ref}}\right)^2}$ [K]
   - $\text{Interior\_RMSE}_i$: Evaluated over cells $j \in [1, N_y-2], k \in [1, N_x-2]$ [K]
   - $\text{Boundary\_RMSE}_i$: Evaluated over cells adjacent to at least one domain boundary [K]
   - $\text{MaxAE}_i = \max_{j, k} \left|T_{i, j, k}^{\text{pred}} - T_{i, j, k}^{\text{ref}}\right|$ [K]
   - $\text{Rel\_}L_{2, i} = \frac{\|T_i^{\text{pred}} - T_i^{\text{ref}}\|_2}{\|T_i^{\text{ref}}\|_2} \times 100\%$ [%]

2. **Physical Boundary Heat Flows and Energy Balance**:
   - $Q_{\text{gen}, i} = \sum_{j=0}^{N_y-1} \sum_{k=0}^{N_x-1} q_{i, j, k} \Delta x \Delta y W_{\text{slab}}$ [W]
   - Reconstructed boundary heat flows ($Q_{\text{left}}, Q_{\text{right}}, Q_{\text{bottom}}, Q_{\text{top}}$) evaluated using exact cell-centred FVM face fluxes:
     - Dirichlet West face ($x=0$, $\mathbf{n} = (-1, 0)$): $Q_{\text{left}, i} = \sum_j \frac{2 k_{i, j, 0}}{\Delta x} (T_{i, j, 0}^{\text{pred}} - T_{\text{dir}, i, j}) \Delta y W_{\text{slab}}$
     - Dirichlet East face ($x=L_x$, $\mathbf{n} = (+1, 0)$): $Q_{\text{right}, i} = \sum_j \frac{2 k_{i, j, N_x-1}}{\Delta x} (T_{i, j, N_x-1}^{\text{pred}} - T_{\text{dir}, i, j}) \Delta y W_{\text{slab}}$
     - Dirichlet South face ($y=0$, $\mathbf{n} = (0, -1)$): $Q_{\text{bottom}, i} = \sum_k \frac{2 k_{i, 0, k}}{\Delta y} (T_{i, 0, k}^{\text{pred}} - T_{\text{dir}, i, k}) \Delta x W_{\text{slab}}$
     - Dirichlet North face ($y=L_y$, $\mathbf{n} = (0, +1)$): $Q_{\text{top}, i} = \sum_k \frac{2 k_{i, N_y-1, k}}{\Delta y} (T_{i, N_y-1, k}^{\text{pred}} - T_{\text{dir}, i, k}) \Delta x W_{\text{slab}}$
     - Neumann faces: Imposed flux density multiplied by face area.
   - $Q_{\text{out}, i} = Q_{\text{left}, i} + Q_{\text{right}, i} + Q_{\text{bottom}, i} + Q_{\text{top}, i}$ [W]
   - **Signed Net Imbalance**: $\Delta Q_{\text{signed}, i} = Q_{\text{out}, i} - Q_{\text{gen}, i}$ [W]. Positive indicates heat loss; negative indicates heat accumulation.
   - **Absolute Net Imbalance**: $\Delta Q_{\text{abs}, i} = |Q_{\text{out}, i} - Q_{\text{gen}, i}|$ [W]
   - **Bounded Relative Imbalance**: $\text{Rel\_Imbalance}_i = \frac{\Delta Q_{\text{abs}, i}}{\max(|Q_{\text{gen}, i}|, |Q_{\text{out}, i}|, 10^{-12})} \times 100\%$ [%]. Bounded in $[0, 200\%]$.

3. **Discrete PDE Residual**:
   - $\|R_i\|_2 = \sqrt{\sum_{j, k} R_{i, j, k}^2}$ [W], where $R_{i, j, k} = \sum_{\text{faces}} \Phi_{\text{face}} - q_{i, j, k} V_P$.

### 4.2 Dataset-Level Aggregation Protocol

For a dataset partition of $N$ samples:
- **Sample Mean ($\mu$)**: Primary reporting scalar $\mu(X) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N X_i$.
- **Sample Standard Deviation ($s$)**: $s(X) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N-1} \sum_{i=1}^N (X_i - \mu(X))^2}$ with Bessel's correction ($\text{ddof}=1$).
- **Median**: 50th percentile for outlier-robust analysis.
- **Pooled Dataset RMSE**: $\text{RMSE}_{\text{pooled}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \text{RMSE}_i^2}$. Must be explicitly distinguished from sample mean RMSE ($\text{RMSE}_{\text{pooled}} \ge \mu(\text{RMSE})$ by Jensen's inequality).
- **Subgroup Filtering**: Subgroups must be partitioned **strictly by physical parameter mask**:
  - `High-Contrast Inclusions`: $\max(k)/\min(k) \ge 20.0$ ($N=153$).
  - `Short-Correlation GRF`: $\max(k)/\min(k) < 20.0$ ($N=47$).
  - Never use index array slicing (`[:153]` or `[153:200]`).

## 7. Audit of Historical Claims

Based on this comprehensive reconciliation, historical claims across milestones are categorized into **Supported**, **Qualified**, and **Withdrawn**:

### 7.1 Supported Claims (Fully Validated)
1. **The $59,591.9\text{ W}$ Baseline Anomaly**:
   - *Claim*: The baseline digital twin exhibits an extreme boundary flux imbalance of $\approx 59.6\text{ kW}$ on short-correlation GRFs (Milestones 05B, 06A, 06B, 08A).
   - *Status*: **SUPPORTED**. The frozen 5-member deep ensemble average prediction produces exactly $59,591.9\text{ W}$ ($14.26\%$ relative $L_2$) on the 47 canonical GRF samples.
2. **Mitigation of GRF Flux Imbalance by Face-Consistent Loss (A1)**:
   - *Claim*: Training with face-consistent boundary loss ($w=0.05$) reduces GRF flux imbalance from $90.2\text{ kW}$ to $35.5\text{ kW}$ ($60.7\%$ reduction) and reduces GRF temperature error from $72.6\text{ K}$ to $36.0\text{ K}$ ($50.4\%$ reduction) (Milestone 08B).
   - *Status*: **SUPPORTED**. Verified exact to numerical precision on Candidate A1 seed 42.
3. **Superior In-Distribution Accuracy of Baseline A0**:
   - *Claim*: The unconstrained supervised FNO-2D achieves sub-Kelvin error ($0.923\text{ K}$, $0.283\%$) on in-distribution operating envelopes and holdout calibration sets.
   - *Status*: **SUPPORTED**. A0 strictly dominates all physics-regularized and boundary-corrected candidates in-distribution.

### 7.2 Qualified Claims (Require Contextual Precision)
1. **Reporting of GRF Metrics in Milestone 08B.1**:
   - *Claim*: Candidate A0 exhibits $24,301.2\text{ W}$ and A1 exhibits $9,595.7\text{ W}$ on the GRF subgroup.
   - *Qualification*: These numbers reflect index slice `[153:200]`, which was composed of $76.6\%$ High-Contrast Inclusions and only $23.4\%$ GRF samples. The true canonical GRF imbalances are $90,192.2\text{ W}$ (A0) and $35,471.2\text{ W}$ (A1).
2. **A2 Reduction of GRF Imbalance**:
   - *Claim*: Boundary-Aware FNO (A2) reduces GRF flux imbalance by $47\%$ (Milestone 08B).
   - *Qualification*: Supported for extrapolation projection ($47,761.2\text{ W}$ vs. $90,192.2\text{ W}$). However, when exact single-cell FVM balance is enforced (A2\*), the imbalance rises to $75,977.1\text{ W}$ because local single-cell balance does not guarantee global domain flux matching without full boundary-layer solving.

### 7.3 Withdrawn Claims
1. **Proposal to Promote A1 to Production**:
   - *Withdrawn*: The proposal in Milestone 08B to promote A1 based on OOD GRF flux reduction is formally withdrawn. Sacrificing in-distribution accuracy by $17\times$ ($0.92\text{ K} \to 15.6\text{ K}$) violates core digital twin safety invariants, especially since the hybrid screening engine already safely diverts OOD GRF cases to numerical FVM fallback.
2. **Equivalence of Linear Extrapolation to Exact FVM Boundaries**:
   - *Withdrawn*: Describing linear face extrapolation ($1.5 T_0 - 0.5 T_1 = T_{\text{dir}}$) as an "exact FVM boundary condition" is mathematically invalid under non-zero heat generation ($q \neq 0$), where it induces an $O(\Delta x^2)$ bias of $+\frac{3 q \Delta x^2}{8 k}$.
